# LOGOS Alpha 0.25 — reflective monitoring and native scheduling

Based on Alpha 0.24 consolidated (GitHub db7024ab). The three owner-supplied reflective design inputs are preserved under design_inputs; their prototype is not executed by the application. The adapted reflection.py is connected to Strategy, Knowledge, Collective and Planner. It stores at most 1000 recent events, strips raw questions and proof trees, and marks reports observational and unauthenticated. Counts are event counts, not distinct incidents (strategy and inference can both record one conflict). Standalone Knowledge/Collective instances without a monitor are not instrumented. Database physical file size and existing non-monitor research history are not capped by retention.

ResearchScheduler stores up to 1000 recurring native objectives; explicit tick runs at most eight due jobs, priority then due date; skipped periods are coalesced. It uses Planner.resume so hypotheses can be refuted by opposing native premises, not automatically promoted. Cancellation is durable. No background daemon, provider calls, arbitrary code execution, or external fetches. Single writer only; a crash between investigation persistence and updating the next due time may cause re-evaluation. No exactly-once guarantee. Priority starvation is possible. Existing evidence policy and revocation retained; no independent source reliability validation.
